[h1] No Vehicle Damage Complete [/h1] 
[h2] Works With 1.26+ [/h2]

[b]This Mod Does not effect server performance[/b]

[h1] Features [/h1]

[h3] 🛡️ Complete Vehicle Protection [/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]No Collision Damage[/b]: Vehicles take no damage from crashing into walls, trees, or other cars.
[*] [b]Bullet & Explosion Immunity[/b]: Vehicles are immune to gunfire, grenades, and melee attacks.
[*] [b]Indestructible Tires[/b]: Tires will automatically repair themselves if damaged.
[*] [b]Protected Components[/b]: Engine, radiator, and fuel tank cannot be ruined.
[*] [b]Visual Protection[/b]: Vehicles always look pristine (No shattered glass or ruined parts).
[/list]

[h3] ♾️ Infinite Maintenance [/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Infinite Fuel[/b]: Fuel tank automatically refills.
[*] [b]Infinite Fluids[/b]: Oil, Coolant, and Brake Fluid automatically keep themselves topped up.
[*] [b]Infinite Battery[/b]: Battery never drains and stays at max charge.
[/list]

[h3] 🤸 Flip Vehicle Action [/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Easy Recovery[/b]: Walk up to any flipped vehicle (upside down or on its side).
[*] [b]Context Action[/b]: A "Flip Vehicle" action appears in the interaction menu.
[*] [b]Safe Flipping[/b]: The car is gently uprighted and lifted slightly to prevent clipping.
[/list]

[h3] 🏊 Environmental Protection [/h3]
[list]
[*] [b]Waterproof Engines[/b]: Engines do not take damage when submerged.
[*] [b]No Drowning[/b]: Players inside vehicles will not drown underwater.
[*] [b]Safe Collisions[/b]: Players and Zombies do not take damage/impact from vehicle collisions.
[/list]

[h1] ⚙️ Server Configuration [/h1]
The mod creates a JSON configuration file on the first server start:
[code]$profile:WLM_NoVehicleDamageComplete/settings.json[/code]

You can toggle features on/off individually:
[code]
{
    "EnableNoVehicleDamage": 1,
    "EnableInfiniteFuel": 1,
    "EnableInfiniteBattery": 1,
    "EnableIndestructibleTires": 1,
    "EnableFlipVehicle": 1,
    "EnablePlayerDrowningProtection": 1,
    "EnablePlayerCollisionProtection": 1,
    "EnableZombieCollisionProtection": 1,
    "EnableVehicleWaterDamageProtection": 1
}
[/code]
